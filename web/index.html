    About the data

    "Free" means no class is scheduled. Bookings, events and exams aren't included, and rooms with no classes all term aren't listed. Seat counts marked "est." come from the largest class held in the room. Unofficial; not affiliated with the University of Waterloo. Times are Toronto time.
